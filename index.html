About

Yuxuan Cai is a third‑class honours bachelor student majoring in Data Science and Big Data Technology at Xi’an‑Liverpool University. Proficient in Python, SQL and mainstream machine‑learning frameworks including Scikit‑learn, XGBoost, LightGBM and LangGraph, with hands‑on experience in full‑stack analytics platform development, RAG‑based AI agent construction and competitive data‑mining modeling.

He has delivered end‑to‑end projects covering asynchronous task scheduling, vector knowledge base, time‑series predictive modeling and spatial‑geographic data analysis, with solid capability to avoid data leakage and build reproducible evaluation workflows. Holding an IELTS 6.5 score, he is capable of reading English technical documentation and eager to pursue data‑science‑related internship opportunities.
